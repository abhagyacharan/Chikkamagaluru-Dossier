Safety & Quick Tips

Read This Before Day 1

Nothing dramatic - just the stuff that actually ruins a monsoon trek if you skip it.

Drink & Drive - No

You've got beers planned both nights and a 5-person car doing all the driving. Decide who's off-duty before the first bottle opens, not after. No exceptions.

Leeches

Real and common on the Hebbe, Jhari, and Baba Budangiri trails in July. Carry salt or tobacco powder, wear full-cover shoes, tuck your pants into your socks.

Waterfall Currents

Monsoon flow is strong and rocks are slick. Stick to marked pool areas at Hebbe and Jhari, don't chase the "better spot" past the ropes.

Book Ahead

Mullayanagiri entry passes and both jeep rides (Jhari, Hebbe) sell out on monsoon weekends. Lock all three a day or two in advance.

Mountain Roads

Baba Budangiri and Kemmangundi roads are narrow, misty, and slippery - hairpins with low visibility. Go slow, use fog lights, don't overtake blind.

Network

Patchy signal past Baba Budangiri and around Kemmangundi. Screenshot this page or save it offline before you lose signal, and share a live location before you head up.

Fuel Up Early

Petrol bunks thin out past Chikmagalur town. Top up before Day 2 and Day 3 drives, not when the tank's already low.

Pack For Wet

Quick-dry clothes, a dry bag for phones/wallets, a spare pair of socks per person. Everything gets wet at least once - plan for it.

FAQ

Questions That'll Come Up

Is Hebbe Falls jeep booking mandatory, or can we just drive up?

Private vehicles aren't allowed on the Hebbe Falls track - it's jeep-only from the designated entry point. Budget the ₹708/head and go early to avoid a long queue for jeeps.

What if it's raining too hard to trek Mullayanagiri or Z Point?

Both spots can close or get genuinely unsafe in heavy downpour - visibility drops and the steps/trails get slick. Check conditions with the dorm the morning of, and have Baba Budangiri's scenic drive or the coffee estate walk as a fallback since those need less trail exposure.

Do we need cash, or is UPI fine everywhere?

UPI works in Chikmagalur town, but jeep counters and small entry points at Jhari, Hebbe, and Z Point are often cash-only, especially in monsoon when card machines act up. Carry enough cash for permits and jeep rides.

Is the water at Jhari and Hebbe safe to get into?

Shallow marked pool areas are generally fine, but monsoon current is strong. Don't swim past the marked zones, and skip it entirely if the falls look unusually swollen that day - locals/jeep drivers will usually tell you if it's a bad day.

How much fuel should we actually budget for the drive?

~1,500km total across the trip. At a blended ~13.8km/l for the XUV 3XO (15 highway / 12 city) and diesel around ₹92/l, that's roughly ₹10,000 in fuel plus ~₹1,200 in tolls - split five ways, that's the ₹2,240/head in the ledger above.

Do we need any special permit to drive a Telangana-registered car into Karnataka?

No extra permit is needed for a private, non-commercial vehicle crossing state lines - this only applies to commercial vehicles. Just make sure FASTag is loaded, since Karnataka's highway tolls run on the same national system.

What's the nearest hospital if something goes wrong on a trek?

Chikmagalur town has a district hospital and a few private clinics, roughly 20-40 minutes from most of these trek points depending on traffic and road conditions. Save the district hospital's location offline before you lose signal near Baba Budangiri or Kemmangundi.

Any wildlife we should be careful of on these trails?

The Baba Budangiri and Mullayanagiri range borders reserve forest, so keep an eye out for the occasional wild boar or snake on misty trails, especially early morning. Stick to marked paths, don't wander off after dark, and keep food packed away at the dorm.

Are there ATMs in Chikmagalur town?

Yes, a handful in the main town area, but none near Baba Budangiri, Kemmangundi, or the falls. Withdraw what you need before Day 2 - don't count on finding one mid-trek.
